How this came to be

This design system has been cultivated over the course of 10 or so years, but it really started showing sprouts in 2020 when I transitioned my artistic identity from Jahn Rome to Jackintheway. That’s when the Wayspace name and early color scheme arrived, and it’s been a steady refinement ever since.

  1. 2021

    The tool pack

    Six objects, with each one representing a different quality. Building these prototypes helped me to develop the adventurous, playful, yet tangible feel that Wayspace would come to have.

    • 2022

      Wayspace, the album

      This is the culmination of years of design and music growth, arriving here in Wayspace. This is an arrival, a homecoming. I built out the dimension with characters, and they welcome us!

      • 2023

        The Wayspace podcast cover

        I brought the puzzle pieces forward, rebuilt the wordmark out of those same blocks, and flattened the ground to blue.
