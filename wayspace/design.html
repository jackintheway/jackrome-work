How this design system got here, told in order, and the flyers, logos and covers made along the way.

How this came to look like this

I have been drawing this for about ten years. It started showing sprouts in 2020, when the name Wayspace and the colours arrived at the same time, and the outline has barely moved since. Here it is in order. Each one came out of the one before it.

  1. 2021

    The tool pack

    Six objects, each one standing for a quality. I drew them and animated them.

    They were prototypes. The outline, the flat fill and the colours this site runs on were worked out here first, on a boombox and a pair of goggles.

      Point at one to play it. On a phone, tap.

    • 2022

      Wayspace, the album

      Twelve tracks, twelve covers, and one image they all belong to.

      The tools came with me. The boombox is on the cover for Salt and the helmet is on Little Things, and the rest are scattered through the set for whoever goes looking.

      • 2023

        The Wayspace podcast cover

        The puzzle pieces come forward, the wordmark gets built out of the same blocks, and the ground goes flat blue.

        This is the most recent one, and it is where most of what you are looking at right now came from.

      • Now

        This site
