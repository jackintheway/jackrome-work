Jack's Commentary:

I'm glad to say I am in a healthy place now, quite healthy. With mindful practices, medication, and loved ones and community support, I am in a healthier place than I've ever been. But it felt right to have this be the first song lyrics we do here. This is quintessential conversation between the one who needs healing and within, the one who heals.

Much of my music as I grew up served that purpose, the way that I could send messages to myself when I needed help, whether I was reaching to myself for help, or trying to give myself help, or really, it was always both.
