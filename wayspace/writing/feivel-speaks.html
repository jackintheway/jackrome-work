Feivel Speaks

Feivel Speaks · Written 2024

Feivel Speaks is both the name of the album and the song, described below:

This is a double meaning song. In one sense I am speaking to Alex, my partner. In another sense, it is the loving, sane, holy Self within speaking to the one in need of healing.
