Orchard

Lyric

You have entered the Orchard. This is where the music lives.

Every file in this folder contains lyrics written by Jack Rome as the artist Jackintheway, accompanied by his own commentary and reflections on the song. These are not performance artifacts or liner notes. They are what Jack calls eternal conversations: songs that reach inward for support and offer it outward across time, in the tradition of the Bhagavad Gita and the Gospels.

Read these when you want to understand Jack's creative and spiritual voice at its most distilled. The Orchard has been cultivated over many years. The fruit here can be returned to season after season.

Commentary sections are sometimes Jack's own words, added after the fact, and sometimes commentary from AI collaborators, historically and primarily Claude, about notes suggested to add, giving context for why a song was written and what it is really about beneath the surface. Each collaborator's commentary is attributed by name.
